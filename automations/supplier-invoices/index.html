Supplier invoice automation

An emailed PDF invoice is filed, logged and sent to the right manager for approval, without anyone retyping it.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Invoices arrive by email, someone saves the PDF, types the details into a spreadsheet and forwards it to a manager. Then the invoice waits in an inbox, and nobody is sure whether it was approved.

How it works

  1. Email arrivesA supplier sends an invoice to your invoices mailbox.
  2. PDF is filedThe PDF is saved to the right SharePoint folder, named and dated.
  3. Row is loggedA row is added to the invoice register with the status Pending.
  4. Manager approvesThe manager approves or rejects with one click, from email or Teams.
  5. Recorded and notifiedThe register records who decided and when, and accounts is notified.

What you get

  • An invoice register with every invoice and its status
  • PDFs stored in one place, named the same way
  • A record of who approved what and when
  • A failure alert if anything goes wrong
