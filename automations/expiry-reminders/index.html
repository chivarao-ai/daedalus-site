Office and IT

Contract and licence expiry reminders

Contracts, licences and permits send a reminder to their owner well before they run out.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

A licence, insurance policy or permit expires and nobody notices until something stops working or a fine arrives.

How it works

  1. Register is keptEach contract, licence or permit is listed with its expiry date and owner.
  2. Daily checkEvery morning the automation checks what is due to expire.
  3. Owner is remindedThe owner is emailed 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry, and again if it lapses.
  4. Weekly overviewThe manager receives one list of everything expiring in the next 60 days.

What you get

  • One register of everything that expires
  • Reminders at the intervals you choose
  • A weekly overview for management
