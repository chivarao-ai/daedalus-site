Sales and customers

Customer request tracking automation

A shared mailbox becomes a tracker with an owner and a due date on every request, and every customer gets an acknowledgement.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Customer emails land in a shared inbox where several people read them, and some are never answered because everyone thought someone else would reply.

How it works

  1. Email arrivesA customer writes to your info or support mailbox.
  2. Request is loggedIt becomes a request in the tracker, sorted into a category.
  3. Owner is assignedThe right person is assigned and notified in Teams or by email.
  4. Customer is answeredThe customer receives an acknowledgement with a reference number.
  5. Overdue is flaggedOpen requests past their due date are sent to the manager every day.

What you get

  • One list of every customer request and who owns it
  • Reference numbers for customers
  • A daily list of overdue requests
