Office and IT

Digital forms that replace paper

Inspection, visitor and incident forms are filled in on a phone, stored where you can find them, and escalated at once when something is wrong.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Paper forms are filled in, filed in a binder and never looked at again. When something goes wrong, the right person hears about it days later.

How it works

  1. Form is filled inStaff complete the form on a phone, opened from a QR code where it is used.
  2. Answers are storedEvery answer is saved to a SharePoint list you can filter and search.
  3. Problems escalateA failed check or serious incident alerts the supervisor straight away.
  4. Record copyOptionally, a PDF copy of each form is saved for your records.

What you get

  • Searchable records instead of binders
  • Immediate alerts for failures and incidents
  • A QR code for each form location
