About us

About Daedalus Automations

Daedalus Automations is a workflow automation service based in Paramaribo, Suriname. We build automations inside Microsoft 365 for firms in Suriname and the Caribbean.

Who is behind it

Daedalus Automations was founded in 2026 by Chivarao, an IT professional in Paramaribo. He has worked in IT support since 2014 and builds workflows with Power Automate, SharePoint and DocuSign.

He trained in ICT at NATIN and in System and Network Engineering at UNASAT.

Why we started

Most firms already pay for Microsoft 365, but their paperwork still moves by hand: retyped into spreadsheets, forwarded by email and chased by phone.

The tools to automate that work are often already included in what they pay for. What is missing is someone to set it up properly, at a fixed price, and support it afterwards. That is the gap Daedalus Automations fills.

How we work

  • Fixed prices, agreed before we start
  • Your documents stay in your own Microsoft 365
  • Everything we build is owned by your account and documented
  • Support after handover, including 30 days of free fixes
  • We work in English and Dutch

Why the name Daedalus

In Greek myth, Daedalus was the craftsman who designed the labyrinth. Our logo is a small labyrinth: the paths your paperwork takes, mapped and made to run on their own.
