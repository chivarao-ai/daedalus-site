Expense claim automation

Staff submit a claim with a photo of the receipt, a manager approves it, and accounts gets a logged claim ready to pay.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Paper receipts get lost, claims arrive in different formats, and accounts has to chase both the employee and the manager before anything is paid.

How it works

  1. Claim is submittedThe employee fills in a short form on a phone and adds a photo of the receipt.
  2. Receipt is storedThe receipt is saved to SharePoint, named with the date and the employee.
  3. Claim is loggedThe claim is added to the expense register with the status Pending.
  4. Manager approvesThe manager sees the amount, category and receipt, and decides in one click.
  5. Ready to payAccounts is notified of approved claims; rejected claims go back with comments.

What you get

  • One expense register with every claim and its receipt
  • No more lost paper receipts
  • Approvals on record
  • A clear list of claims ready to pay
