Delivery paperwork automation

Delivery notes and proof of delivery are filed against the right order, so you can find them in seconds when an auditor or customer asks.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Signed delivery notes end up in folders, drawers and phones. When a customer disputes a delivery, finding the proof takes hours.

How it works

  1. Scan is addedA scanned delivery note is dropped into a SharePoint folder, named with the order number.
  2. Order is foundThe automation looks up the matching order in your order list.
  3. Filed and updatedThe scan is filed under the order, and the order is marked Delivered with the date.
  4. Owner is toldThe order owner is notified. Scans with no matching order go to a clerk to check.

What you get

  • Every proof of delivery linked to its order
  • Orders marked delivered automatically
  • A short list of unmatched scans to check by hand
