New staff onboarding automation

One form starts the checklist: account, equipment, documents to sign, and a reminder for each owner until it is done.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

A new employee arrives and the laptop is not ready, the account does not exist, and the contract is still unsigned. Everyone assumed someone else had it.

How it works

  1. Starter is addedHR adds the new employee, start date, role and manager to one list.
  2. Tasks are createdEvery task from your onboarding checklist is created with an owner and a due date.
  3. Owners are toldEach owner receives their tasks for this starter.
  4. Reminders followOverdue tasks are sent to their owner every working morning.
  5. Manager confirmsThe manager is told when everything is ready.

What you get

  • A standard onboarding checklist you control
  • Owners and due dates on every task
  • Daily reminders, so nothing is forgotten
