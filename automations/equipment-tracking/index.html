Office and IT

Equipment and asset tracking automation

Laptops, phones and tools are logged when issued and returned, so you always know who has what.

Book a free 30-minute callSee all demos

The problem

Equipment goes out with a verbal agreement. When someone leaves, nobody knows what they should hand back.

How it works

  1. Form is submittedStaff record an issue or return on a form, opened from a QR code on the asset label.
  2. Asset is updatedThe asset list shows the new holder and date, or marks it back in stock.
  3. History is loggedEvery issue and return is added to the asset log.
  4. Receipt is sentThe employee receives a receipt of what they hold or returned.

What you get

  • An up-to-date list of every asset and who holds it
  • A full history per asset
  • A monthly list for each manager of their team’s equipment
